Each workflow below is complete and ready to run. Open one to see what it does,
then download it and load it with [Import](/guides/use-an-example/), or run it in
your own copy. They go from the simplest to the most involved, so reading them in
order shows you every kind of workflow you can build.

| Workflow | What it shows |
|---|---|
| [Live Price Check](#live-price-check) | A single agent with one data tool |
| [Two-Step Pipeline](#two-step-pipeline) | One agent gathers, another analyzes |
| [Draft, Review, Approve](#draft-review-approve) | A drafting loop with a reviewer |
| [Parallel Research Tracks](#parallel-research-tracks) | Two tracks that split and rejoin |
| [News and Social Pulse](#news-and-social-pulse) | Collect news and posts, then score the mood |
| [A Team of Agents](#a-team-of-agents) | A planner hands work to a group working in parallel |
| [Self-Checking Loop](#self-checking-loop) | Nested loops that harden the result |

<Aside type="note">
Every example defaults to the **OpenRouter** provider. The data sources they use are public and need no key: OKX for prices, the ECB for macro data, and Guardian, Lemmy, and Mastodon for news and social posts. See [Set up your keys and data sources](/guides/configure-providers/).
</Aside>

## Live Price Check

**Download:** [`simple_price_check.yaml`](/examples/simple_price_check.yaml)

One Market Analyst agent fetches the current Bitcoin price and writes a short,
evidence-cited summary. It keeps trying until it has real numbers to cite. This is
the smallest complete workflow: one agent, one tool, one clear finish line. It is
the workflow used in the [tutorial](/tutorials/first-workflow/).

**Uses:** OKX prices.

## Two-Step Pipeline

**Download:** [`sequential_analysis.yaml`](/examples/sequential_analysis.yaml)

A Data Gatherer collects the Bitcoin price and hands it to a Senior Analyst, which
turns it into a bullish or bearish read with a risk note. Only the gatherer
touches the price tool, and the analyst works from what the gatherer passed along.
This shows how work flows from one agent to the next.

**Uses:** OKX prices.

## Draft, Review, Approve

**Download:** [`signal_memo_cycle.yaml`](/examples/signal_memo_cycle.yaml)

A pipeline with a built-in revision loop. Data Intake collects the price, a Memo
Drafter writes a trading-signal memo, and a Memo Reviewer either sends back one
revision or approves it. A Memo Finalizer emits the approved memo. This shows how a
workflow can improve its own output before finishing.

**Uses:** OKX prices.

## Parallel Research Tracks

**Download:** [`dual_track_briefing.yaml`](/examples/dual_track_briefing.yaml)

One question, split into two tracks that run side by side: a Market Analyst on the
Bitcoin price and a Macro Analyst on the European Central Bank's key rate. A
Briefing Synthesizer joins both tracks and states the tension between price and
policy as a possibility, not a certainty. This shows branches that fork and rejoin.

**Uses:** OKX prices, ECB policy rate.

## News and Social Pulse

**Download:** [`news_sentiment_digest.yaml`](/examples/news_sentiment_digest.yaml)

A News and Social Collector gathers Bitcoin news and community posts, then a
Sentiment Analyst summarizes them and scores the mood, backed by the posts behind
the score. This shows how to combine fetching and text analysis across agents.

**Uses:** Guardian news, Lemmy and Mastodon posts.

## A Team of Agents

**Download:** [`multi_agent_research.yaml`](/examples/multi_agent_research.yaml)

A Research Planner writes three assignments, one each for market, macro, and news.
A Research Analyst Team runs all three in parallel, and a Briefing Synthesizer
joins their findings into one briefing. This shows how a planner can delegate to a
group of agents working at the same time.

**Uses:** OKX prices, ECB policy rate, Guardian news.

## Self-Checking Loop

**Download:** [`iterative_risk_review.yaml`](/examples/iterative_risk_review.yaml)

The most involved example: a short-term Bitcoin risk verdict built with loops
inside loops. An inner draft-and-critique loop sharpens the read, and an outer loop
sends the work back for more data when the evidence is thin. This shows how loops
can nest to raise the quality of the result.

**Uses:** OKX prices, ECB policy rate.
