## What it does

<CardGrid>
  <Card title="Design it visually, no code" icon="setting">
    Drag agents and data tools onto a canvas and connect them into a flow. What you draw is the workflow. There is nothing separate to configure and nothing to write in code.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Reads backed by real data" icon="magnifier">
    Your agents pull live prices, policy rates, news, and social posts, then show the evidence they used. Every claim traces back to a source you can open and check.
  </Card>
  <Card title="No hand-wavy answers" icon="approve-check">
    Each step passes only when it clears a bar you set: the right data, the required detail, and a quality check. A vague answer does not count as done.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Watch it think, live" icon="rocket">
    Start a run and watch each agent fetch data, reason, and report in real time, one step at a time.
  </Card>
</CardGrid>

## How it works

Three steps take you from an idea to a running research agent.

<Steps>

1. **Design your workflow.** Drop agents and data tools on the canvas and connect them into the flow you want.

2. **Connect your model.** Add your own provider key in Settings. Your keys and your runs stay yours.

3. **Run and watch.** Launch the workflow and watch each agent gather evidence and produce a read, live.

</Steps>
